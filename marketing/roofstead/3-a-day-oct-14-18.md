## The part that actually gets reach (James, 10 min a day)

Posting 3x a day on a new Page with few likes reaches mostly nobody until people engage. These move the
needle:

1. **Invite friends to like the Page** (Page → ⋯ → Invite friends). Roofers you know first.
2. **Share the noon post to your personal profile** and the Twin Bridge Page once a day.
3. **Reply to every comment within the first hour.** Ask a follow-up question so they reply again.
4. **Keep running the paid campaign.** Organic builds trust; the ads bring the clicks.
